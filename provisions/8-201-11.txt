8-201.11 When Plans Are Required.
A PERMIT applicant or PERMIT HOLDER shall submit to the REGULATORY AUTHORITY
properly prepared plans and specifications for review and approval before:
(A) The construction of a FOOD ESTABLISHMENT; Pf
(B) The conversion of an existing structure for use as a FOOD
ESTABLISHMENT; Pf or
(C) The remodeling of a FOOD ESTABLISHMENT or a change of type of FOOD
ESTABLISHMENT or FOOD operation as specified under ¶ 8-302.14(C) if the
REGULATORY AUTHORITY determines that plans and specifications are
necessary to ensure compliance with this Code. Pf
