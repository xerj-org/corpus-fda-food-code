5-102.12 Nondrinking Water.
(A) A nonDRINKING WATER supply shall be used only if its use is APPROVED. P
(B) NonDRINKING WATER shall be used only for nonculinary purposes such as air
conditioning, nonFOOD EQUIPMENT cooling, and fire protection. P
