4-603.15 Washing, Procedures for Alternative Manual Warewashing
Equipment.
If washing in sink compartments or a WAREWASHING machine is impractical such
as when the EQUIPMENT is fixed or the UTENSILS are too large, washing shall be
done by using alternative manual WAREWASHING EQUIPMENT as specified in ¶
4-301.12(C) in accordance with the following procedures:
(A) EQUIPMENT shall be disassembled as necessary to allow access of the
detergent solution to all parts;
(B) EQUIPMENT components and UTENSILS shall be scraped or rough
cleaned to remove FOOD particle accumulation; and
(C) EQUIPMENT and UTENSILS shall be washed as specified under
¶ 4-603.14(A).
