4-102.11 Characteristics.
Materials that are used to make SINGLE-SERVICE and SINGLE-USE ARTICLES:
(A) May not:
(1) Allow the migration of deleterious substances, P or
(2) Impart colors, odors, or tastes to FOOD; and
(B) Shall be:
(1) Safe, P and
(2) Clean.
