5-402.13 Conveying Sewage.
SEWAGE shall be conveyed to the point of disposal through an APPROVED sanitary
SEWAGE system or other system, including use of SEWAGE transport vehicles,
waste retention tanks, pumps, pipes, hoses, and connections that are
constructed, maintained, and operated according to LAW.P
