8-402.40 Inspection Order to Gain Access.
If denied access to a FOOD ESTABLISHMENT for an authorized purpose and after
complying with § 8-402.20, the REGULATORY AUTHORITY may issue, or apply for
the issuance of, an inspection order to gain access as provided in LAW.
