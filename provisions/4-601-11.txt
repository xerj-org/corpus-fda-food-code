4-601.11 Equipment, Food-Contact Surfaces, Nonfood-Contact Surfaces,
and Utensils.
(A) EQUIPMENT FOOD-CONTACT SURFACES and UTENSILS shall be clean to sight and
touch. Pf
(B) The FOOD-CONTACT SURFACES of cooking EQUIPMENT and pans shall be kept
free of encrusted grease deposits and other soil accumulations.
(C) NonFOOD-CONTACT SURFACES of EQUIPMENT shall be kept free of an
accumulation of dust, dirt, FOOD residue, and other debris.
