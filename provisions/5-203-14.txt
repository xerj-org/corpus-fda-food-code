5-203.14 Backflow Prevention Device, When Required.
A PLUMBING SYSTEM shall be installed to preclude backflow of a solid, liquid, or
gas contaminant into the water supply system at each point of use at the FOOD
ESTABLISHMENT, including on a hose bibb if a hose is attached or on a hose bibb if
a hose is not attached and backflow prevention is required by LAW, by:
(A) Providing an air gap as specified under § 5-202.13 P; or
(B) Installing an APPROVED backflow prevention device as specified under
§ 5-202.14. P
