6-202.110 Outdoor Refuse Areas, Curbed and Graded to Drain.
Outdoor REFUSE areas shall be constructed in accordance with LAW and shall be
curbed and graded to drain to collect and dispose of liquid waste that results from
the REFUSE and from cleaning the area and waste receptacles.
