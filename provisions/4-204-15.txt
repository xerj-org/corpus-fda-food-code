4-204.15 Bearings and Gear Boxes, Leakproof.
EQUIPMENT containing bearings and gears that require lubricants shall be
designed and constructed so that the lubricant cannot leak, drip, or be forced into
FOOD or onto FOOD-CONTACT SURFACES.
