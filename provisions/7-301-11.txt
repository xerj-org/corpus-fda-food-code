7-301.11 Separation.
POISONOUS or TOXIC MATERIALS shall be stored and displayed for retail sale so
they can not contaminate FOOD, EQUIPMENT, UTENSILS, LINENS, and SINGLE-
SERVICE and SINGLE-USE ARTICLES by:
(A) Separating the POISONOUS or TOXIC MATERIALS by spacing or
partitioning; P and
(B) Locating the POISONOUS OR TOXIC MATERIALS in an area that is not
above FOOD, EQUIPMENT, UTENSILS, LINENS, and SINGLE-SERVICE or SINGLE-
USE ARTICLES. P
