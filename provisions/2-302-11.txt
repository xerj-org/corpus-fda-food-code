2-302.11 Maintenance.
(A) FOOD EMPLOYEES shall keep their fingernails trimmed, filed, and maintained
so the edges and surfaces are cleanable and not rough. Pf
(B) Unless wearing intact gloves in good repair, a FOOD EMPLOYEE may not wear
fingernail polish or artificial fingernails when working with exposed FOOD. Pf
