3-202.110 Juice Treated.
Pre-packaged juice shall:
(A) Be obtained from a processor with a HACCP system as specified in 21
CFR Part 120 Hazard Analysis and Critical Control (HACCP) Systems; Pf
and
(B) Be obtained pasteurized or otherwise treated to attain a 5-log
reduction of the most resistant microorganism of public health significance
as specified in 21 CFR Part 120.24 Process Controls. P
