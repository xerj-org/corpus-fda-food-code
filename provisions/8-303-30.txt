8-303.30 Denial of Application for Permit, Notice.
If an application for a PERMIT to operate is denied, the REGULATORY AUTHORITY
shall provide the applicant with a notice that includes:
(A) The specific reasons and Code citations for the PERMIT denial;
(B) The actions, if any, that the applicant must take to qualify for a PERMIT;
and
(C) Advisement of the applicant's right of appeal and the process and time
frames for appeal that are provided in LAW.
