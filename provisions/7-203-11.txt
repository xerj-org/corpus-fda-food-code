7-203.11 Poisonous or Toxic Material Containers.
A container previously used to store POISONOUS OR TOXIC MATERIALS may not be
used to store, transport, or dispense FOOD, EQUIPMENT, UTENSILS, LINENS, SINGLE-
SERVICE or SINGLE-USE ARTICLES . P
