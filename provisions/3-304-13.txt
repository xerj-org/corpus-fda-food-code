3-304.13 Linens and Napkins, Use Limitation.
LINENS, such as cloth napkins, may not be used in contact with FOOD unless they
are used to line a container for the service of FOODS and the LINENS and napkins
are replaced each time the container is refilled for a new CONSUMER.
