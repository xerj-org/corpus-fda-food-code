2-403.11 Handling Prohibition.
(A) Except as specified in ¶ (B) of this section, FOOD EMPLOYEES may not care for
or handle animals that may be present such as patrol dogs, SERVICE ANIMALS, or
pets that are allowed as specified in Subparagraphs 6-501.115(B)(2) - (5) and
