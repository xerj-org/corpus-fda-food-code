4-203.12 Temperature Measuring Devices, Ambient Air and Water.
(A) Ambient air and water TEMPERATURE MEASURING DEVICES that are scaled in
Celsius or dually scaled in Celsius and Fahrenheit shall be designed to be easily
readable and accurate to ±1.5oC in the intended range of use. Pf
(B) Ambient air and water TEMPERATURE MEASURING DEVICES that are scaled only
in Fahrenheit shall be accurate to ±3oF in the intended range of use. Pf
