2-401.13 Use of Bandages, Finger Cots, or Finger Stalls
If used, an impermeable cover such as a bandage, finger cot or finger stall
located on the wrist, hand or finger of a FOOD EMPLOYEE working with exposed
FOOD shall be covered with a single-use glove.
