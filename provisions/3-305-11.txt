3-305.11 Food Storage.
(A) Except as specified in ¶¶ (B) and (C) of this section, FOOD shall be protected
from contamination by storing the FOOD:
(1) In a clean, dry location;
(2) Where it is not exposed to splash, dust, or other contamination; and
(3) At least 15 cm (6 inches) above the floor.
(B) FOOD in packages and working containers may be stored less than 15 cm (6
inches) above the floor on case lot handling EQUIPMENT as specified under §
4-204.122.
(C) Pressurized BEVERAGE containers, cased FOOD in waterproof containers such
as bottles or cans, and milk containers in plastic crates may be stored on a floor
that is clean and not exposed to floor moisture.
