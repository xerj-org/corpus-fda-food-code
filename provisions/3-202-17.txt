3-202.17 Shellstock, Condition.
When received by a FOOD ESTABLISHMENT, SHELLSTOCK shall be reasonably free
of mud, dead SHELLSTOCK, and SHELLSTOCK with broken shells. Dead
SHELLSTOCK with badly broken shells shall be discarded.
