3-401.15 Manufacturer Cooking Instructions.
(A) Commercially PACKAGED FOOD that bears a manufacturer’s cooking
instructions shall be cooked according to those instructions before use in READY-
TO-EAT FOODS or offered in unPACKAGED form for human consumption, unless the
manufacturer’s instructions specify that the FOOD may be consumed without
cooking. P
(B) FOOD for which the manufacturer has provided information that it has not
been processed to control pathogens, when used in READY-TO-EAT FOODS or
offered for human consumption, shall be cooked according to a time and
temperature appropriate for the FOOD. P
