5-102.11 Standards.
Except as specified under § 5-102.12:
(A) Water from a PUBLIC WATER SYSTEM shall meet 40 CFR 141 - National
Primary Drinking Water Regulations and state DRINKING WATER quality
standards; P and
(B) Water from a nonPUBLIC WATER SYSTEM shall meet state DRINKING
WATER quality standards. P
