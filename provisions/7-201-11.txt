7-201.11 Separation.
Poisonous or toxic materials shall be stored so they can not contaminate food,
equipment, utensils, linens, and single-service and single-use articles by:
(A) Separating the POISONOUS OR TOXIC MATERIALS by spacing or
partitioning; P and
(B) Locating the POISONOUS OR TOXIC MATERIALS in an area that is not
above FOOD, EQUIPMENT, UTENSILS, LINENS, and SINGLE-SERVICE or SINGLE-
USE ARTICLES. This paragraph does not apply to EQUIPMENT and UTENSIL
cleaners and SANITIZERS that are stored in WAREWASHING areas for
availability and convenience if the materials are stored to prevent
contamination of FOOD, EQUIPMENT, UTENSILS, LINENS, and SINGLE-SERVICE
and SINGLE-USE ARTICLES. P
