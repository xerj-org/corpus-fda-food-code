3-501.12 Time/Temperature Control for Safety Food, Slacking.
Frozen TIME/TEMPERATURE CONTROL FOR SAFETY FOOD that is slacked to moderate
the temperature shall be held:
(A) Under refrigeration that maintains the FOOD temperature at 5oC (41oF)
or less; or
(B) At any temperature if the FOOD remains frozen.
