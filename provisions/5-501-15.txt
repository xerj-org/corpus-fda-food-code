5-501.15 Outside Receptacles.
(A) Receptacles and waste handling units for REFUSE, recyclables, and
returnables used with materials containing FOOD residue and used outside the
FOOD ESTABLISHMENT shall be designed and constructed to have tight-fitting lids,
doors, or covers.
(B) Receptacles and waste handling units for REFUSE and recyclables such as an
on-site compactor shall be installed so that accumulation of debris and insect and
rodent attraction and harborage are minimized and effective cleaning is facilitated
around and, if the unit is not installed flush with the base pad, under the unit.
