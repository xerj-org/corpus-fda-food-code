8-103.11 Documentation of Proposed Variance and Justification.
Before a VARIANCE from a requirement of this Code is APPROVED, the information
that shall be provided by the PERSON requesting the VARIANCE and retained in the
REGULATORY AUTHORITY'S file on the FOOD ESTABLISHMENT includes:
(A) A statement of the proposed VARIANCE of the Code requirement citing
relevant Code section numbers;Pf
(B) An analysis of the rationale for how the potential public health HAZARDS
and nuisances addressed by the relevant Code sections will be
alternatively addressed by the proposal; Pf and
(C) A HACCP PLAN if required as specified under ¶ 8-201.13(A) that
includes the information specified under § 8-201.14 as it is relevant to the
VARIANCE requested. Pf
