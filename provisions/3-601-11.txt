3-601.11 Standards of Identity.
PACKAGED FOOD shall comply with standard of identity requirements in 21 CFR
131-169 and 9 CFR 319 Definitions and standards of identity or composition, and
the general requirements in 21 CFR 130 – Food Standards: General and 9 CFR
319 Subpart A – General.
