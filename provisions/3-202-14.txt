3-202.14 Eggs and Milk Products, Pasteurized.
(A) EGG PRODUCTS shall be obtained pasteurized. P
(B) Fluid and dry milk and milk products shall:
(1) Be obtained pasteurized; P and
(2) Comply with GRADE A STANDARDS as specified in LAW. P
(C) Frozen milk products, such as ice cream, shall be obtained pasteurized as
specified in 21 CFR 135 - Frozen desserts. P
(D) Cheese shall be obtained pasteurized unless alternative procedures to
pasteurization are specified in the CFR, such as 21 CFR 133 - Cheeses and
related cheese products, for curing certain cheese varieties. P
