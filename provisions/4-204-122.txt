4-204.122 Case Lot Handling Apparatuses, Moveability.
Apparatuses, such as dollies, pallets, racks, and skids used to store and
transport large quantities of PACKAGED FOODS received from a supplier in a cased
or overwrapped lot, shall be designed to be moved by hand or by conveniently
available apparatuses such as hand trucks and forklifts.
