7-207.12 Refrigerated Medicines, Storage.
Medicines belonging to EMPLOYEES or to children in a day care center that require
refrigeration and are stored in a FOOD refrigerator shall be:
(A) Stored in a package or container and kept inside a covered, leakproof
container that is identified as a container for the storage of medicines; P and
(B) Located so they are inaccessible to children. P
