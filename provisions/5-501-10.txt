5-501.10 Indoor Storage Area.
If located within the FOOD ESTABLISHMENT, a storage area for REFUSE, recyclables,
and returnables shall meet the requirements specified under §§ 6-101.11,
6-201.11 - 6-201.18, 6-202.15, and 6-202.16.
