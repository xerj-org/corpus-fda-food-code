8-501.40 Removal of Exclusions and Restrictions.
The REGULATORY AUTHORITY shall release a FOOD EMPLOYEE, OR CONDITIONAL
EMPLOYEE from RESTRICTION or EXCLUSION according to LAW and the conditions
specified under § 2-201.13.
