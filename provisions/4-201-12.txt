4-201.12 Food Temperature Measuring Devices.
FOOD TEMPERATURE MEASURING DEVICES may not have sensors or stems
constructed of glass, except that thermometers with glass sensors or stems that
are encased in a shatterproof coating such as candy thermometers may be used. P
