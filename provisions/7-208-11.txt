7-208.11 Storage.
First aid supplies that are in a FOOD ESTABLISHMENT for the EMPLOYEES' use shall
be:
(A) Labeled as specified under § 7-101.11; Pf and
(B) Stored in a kit or a container that is located to prevent the
contamination of FOOD, EQUIPMENT, UTENSILS, and LINENS, and SINGLE-
SERVICE and SINGLE-USE ARTICLES. P
