6-501.113 Storing Maintenance Tools.
Maintenance tools such as brooms, mops, vacuum cleaners, and similar items
shall be:
(A) Stored so they do not contaminate FOOD, EQUIPMENT, UTENSILS, LINENS, and
SINGLE-SERVICE and SINGLE-USE ARTICLES; and
(B) Stored in an orderly manner that facilitates cleaning the area used for storing
the maintenance tools.
