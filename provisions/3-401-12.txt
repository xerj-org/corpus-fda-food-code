3-401.12 Microwave Cooking.
Raw animal FOODS cooked in a microwave oven shall be:
(A) Rotated or stirred throughout or midway during cooking to compensate
for uneven distribution of heat;
(B) Covered to retain surface moisture;
(C) Heated to a temperature of at least 74oC (165oF) in all parts of the
FOOD;P and
(D) Allowed to stand covered for 2 minutes after cooking to obtain
temperature equilibrium.
