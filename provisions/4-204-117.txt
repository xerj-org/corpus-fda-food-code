4-204.117 Warewashing Machines, Automatic Dispensing of Detergents
and Sanitizers.
A WAREWASHING machine that is installed after adoption of this Code by the
REGULATORY AUTHORITY, shall be equipped to:
(A) Automatically dispense detergents and SANITIZERS; Pf and
(B) Incorporate a visual means to verify that detergents and SANITIZERS are
delivered or a visual or audible alarm to signal if the detergents and
SANITIZERS are not delivered to the respective washing and SANITIZING
cycles. Pf
