5-402.11 Backflow Prevention.
(A) Except as specified in ¶¶ (B), (C), and (D) of this section, a direct connection
may not exist between the SEWAGE system and a drain originating from
EQUIPMENT in which FOOD, portable EQUIPMENT, or UTENSILS are placed. P
(B) Paragraph (A) of this section does not apply to floor drains that originate in
refrigerated spaces that are constructed as an integral part of the building.
(C) If allowed by LAW, a WAREWASHING machine may have a direct connection
between its waste outlet and a floor drain when the machine is located within 1.5
m (5 feet) of a trapped floor drain and the machine outlet is connected to the inlet
side of a properly vented floor drain trap.
(D) If allowed by LAW, a WAREWASHING or culinary sink may have a direct
connection.
