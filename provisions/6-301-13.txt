6-301.13 Handwashing Aids and Devices, Use Restrictions.
A sink used for FOOD preparation or UTENSIL washing, or a service sink or curbed
cleaning facility used for the disposal of mop water or similar wastes, may not be
provided with the handwashing aids and devices required for a HANDWASHING
SINK as specified under §§ 6-301.11 and 6-301.12 and ¶ 5-501.16(C).
