5-103.11 Capacity.
(A) The water source and system shall be of sufficient capacity to meet the peak
water demands of the FOOD ESTABLISHMENT. Pf
(B) Hot water generation and distribution systems shall be sufficient to meet the
peak hot water demands throughout the FOOD ESTABLISHMENT. Pf
