2-101.11 Assignment
(A) Except as specified in ¶¶ (B) and (C) of this section, the PERMIT HOLDER shall
be the PERSON IN CHARGE or shall designate a PERSON IN CHARGE and shall ensure
that a PERSON IN CHARGE is present at the FOOD ESTABLISHMENT during all hours of
operation.Pf
(B) In a FOOD ESTABLISHMENT with two or more separately PERMITTED departments
that are the legal responsibility of the same PERMIT HOLDER and that are located
on the same PREMISES, the PERMIT HOLDER may, during specific time periods
when food is not being prepared, packaged, or served, designate a single
PERSON IN CHARGE who is present on the PREMISES during all hours of operation,
and who is responsible for each separately PERMITTED FOOD ESTABLISHMENT on
the PREMISES.Pf
(C) This section does not apply to certain types of FOOD ESTABLISHMENTS deemed
by the REGULATORY AUTHORITY to pose minimal risk of causing, or contributing to,
foodborne illness based on the nature of the operation and extent of the FOOD
preparation.Pf
