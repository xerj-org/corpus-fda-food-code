3-304.11 Food Contact with Equipment and Utensils.
FOOD shall only contact surfaces of:
(A) EQUIPMENT and UTENSILS that are cleaned as specified under Part 4-6
of this Code and SANITIZED as specified under Part 4-7 of this Code; P
(B) Single-service and single-use articles; P or
(C) LINENS, such as cloth napkins, as specified under § 3-304.13 that are
laundered as specified under Part 4-8 of this Code. P
