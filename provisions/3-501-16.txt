3-501.16 Time/Temperature Control for Safety Food, Hot and Cold
Holding.
(A) Except during preparation, cooking, or cooling, or when time is used as the
public health control as specified under §3-501.19, and except as specified under
¶ (B) and in ¶ (C) of this section, TIME/TEMPERATURE CONTROL FOR SAFETY FOOD
shall be maintained:
(1) At 57°C (135°F) or above, except that roasts cooked to a temperature
and for a time specified in ¶ 3-401.11(B) or reheated as specified in ¶ 3-
403.11(E) may be held at a temperature of 54oC (130oF) or above; P or
(2) At 5°C (41°F) or less. P
(B) EGGS that have not been treated to destroy all viable Salmonellae shall be
stored in refrigerated EQUIPMENT that maintains an ambient air temperature of
7°C (45°F) or less. P
(C) TIME/TEMPERATURE CONTROL FOR SAFETY FOOD in a homogenous liquid form
may be maintained outside of the temperature control requirements, as specified
under ¶ (A) of this section, while contained within specially designed EQUIPMENT
that complies with the design and construction requirements as specified under ¶
4-204.13(E).
