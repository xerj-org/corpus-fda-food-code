7-202.11 Restriction.
(A) Only those POISONOUS OR TOXIC MATERIALS that are required for the operation
and maintenance of a FOOD ESTABLISHMENT, such as for the cleaning and
SANITIZING of EQUIPMENT and UTENSILS and the control of insects and rodents,
shall be allowed in a FOOD ESTABLISHMENT. Pf
(B) Paragraph (A) of this section does not apply to PACKAGED POISONOUS OR TOXIC
MATERIALS that are for retail sale.
