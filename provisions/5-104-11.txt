5-104.11 System.
Water shall be received from the source through the use of:
(A) An APPROVED public water main; Pf or
(B) One or more of the following that shall be constructed, maintained, and
operated according to LAW: Pf
(1) Nonpublic water main, water pumps, pipes, hoses, connections,
and other appurtenances, Pf
(2) Water transport vehicles, Pf or
(3) Water containers. Pf
