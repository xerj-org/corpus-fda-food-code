1-103.10 Statement.
This Code establishes definitions; sets standards for management and
personnel, FOOD operations, and EQUIPMENT and facilities; and provides for FOOD
ESTABLISHMENT plan review, PERMIT issuance, inspection, EMPLOYEE RESTRICTION,
and PERMIT suspension.
