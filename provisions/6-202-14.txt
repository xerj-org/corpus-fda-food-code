6-202.14 Toilet Rooms, Enclosed.
Except where a toilet room is located outside a food establishment and does not
open directly into the food establishment such as a toilet room that is provided by
the management of a shopping mall, a toilet room located on the PREMISES shall
be completely enclosed and provided with a tight-fitting and self-closing door.
