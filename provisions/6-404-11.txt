6-404.11 Segregation and Location.
Products that are held by the PERMIT HOLDER for credit, redemption, or return to
the distributor, such as damaged, spoiled, or recalled products, shall be
segregated and held in designated areas that are separated from FOOD,
EQUIPMENT, UTENSILS, LINENS, and SINGLE-SERVICE and SINGLE-USE ARTICLES.Pf
