6-202.112 Living or Sleeping Quarters, Separation.
Living or sleeping quarters located on the PREMISES of a FOOD ESTABLISHMENT
such as those provided for lodging registration clerks or resident managers shall
be separated from rooms and areas used for FOOD ESTABLISHMENT operations by
complete partitioning and solid self-closing doors.
