5-402.14 Removing Mobile Food Establishment Wastes.
SEWAGE and other liquid wastes shall be removed from a mobile FOOD
ESTABLISHMENT at an APPROVED waste SERVICING AREA or by a SEWAGE transport
vehicle in such a way that a public health HAZARD or nuisance is not created. Pf
