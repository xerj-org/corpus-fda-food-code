7-204.12 Chemicals for Washing, Treatment, Storage and Processing
Fruits and Vegetables, Criteria.
Chemicals, including those generated on-site, used to wash or peel raw, whole
fruits and vegetables or used in the treatment, storage, and processing of fruits
and vegetables shall:
(A) Be an approved food additive listed for this intended use in 21 CFR
173, P or
(B) Be generally recognized as safe (GRAS) for this intended use, P or
(C) Be the subject of an effective food contact notification for this intended
use (only effective for the manufacturer or supplier identified in the
notification), P and
(D) Meet the requirements in 40 CFR 156 Labeling Requirements for
Pesticide and Devices. P
