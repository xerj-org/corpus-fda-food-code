4-603.11 Dry Cleaning.
(A) If used, dry cleaning methods such as brushing, scraping, and vacuuming
shall contact only SURFACES that are soiled with dry FOOD residues that are not
TIME/TEMPERATURE CONTROL FOR SAFETY FOOD.
(B) Cleaning EQUIPMENT used in dry cleaning FOOD-CONTACT SURFACES may not
be used for any other purpose.
