4-603.14 Wet Cleaning.
(A) EQUIPMENT FOOD-CONTACT SURFACES and UTENSILS shall be effectively
washed to remove or completely loosen soils by using the manual or mechanical
means necessary such as the application of detergents containing wetting agents
and emulsifiers; acid, alkaline, or abrasive cleaners; hot water; brushes; scouring
pads; high-pressure sprays; or ultrasonic devices.
(B) The washing procedures selected shall be based on the type and purpose of
the EQUIPMENT or UTENSIL, and on the type of soil to be removed.
