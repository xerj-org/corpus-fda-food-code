6-202.12 Heating, Ventilating, Air Conditioning System Vents.
Heating, ventilating, and air conditioning systems shall be designed and installed
so that make-up air intake and exhaust vents do not cause contamination of
FOOD, FOOD-CONTACT SURFACES, EQUIPMENT, or UTENSILS.
