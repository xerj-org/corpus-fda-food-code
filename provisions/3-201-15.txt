3-201.15 Molluscan Shellfish.
(A) MOLLUSCAN SHELLFISH shall be obtained from sources according to LAW and
the requirements specified in the U.S. Department of Health and Human
Services, Public Health Service, Food and Drug Administration, National
Shellfish Sanitation Program Guide for the Control of Molluscan Shellfish. P
(B) MOLLUSCAN SHELLFISH received in interstate commerce shall be from sources
that are listed in the Interstate Certified Shellfish Shippers List. P
