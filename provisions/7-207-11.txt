7-207.11 Restriction and Storage.
(A) Except for medicines that are stored or displayed for retail sale, only those
medicines that are necessary for the health of EMPLOYEES shall be allowed in a
FOOD ESTABLISHMENT. Pf
(B) Medicines that are in a FOOD ESTABLISHMENT for the EMPLOYEES' use shall be
labeled as specified under § 7-101.11 and located to prevent the contamination
of FOOD, EQUIPMENT, UTENSILS, LINENS, and SINGLE-SERVICE and SINGLE-USE
ARTICLES.P
