4-502.13 Single-Service and Single-Use Articles, Use Limitation.
(A) SINGLE-SERVICE and SINGLE-USE ARTICLES may not be reused.
(B) The bulk milk container dispensing tube shall be cut on the diagonal leaving
no more than one inch protruding from the chilled dispensing head.
