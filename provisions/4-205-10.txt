4-205.10 Food Equipment, Certification and Classification.
FOOD EQUIPMENT that is certified or classified for sanitation in conformance to a
recognized American National Standard by an American National Standards
Institute (ANSI)-accredited certification program is deemed to comply with Parts
4-1 and 4-2 of this chapter.
