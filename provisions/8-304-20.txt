8-304.20 Permits Not Transferable.
A PERMIT may not be transferred from one PERSON to another PERSON, from one
FOOD ESTABLISHMENT to another, or from one type of operation to another if the
FOOD operation changes from the type of operation specified in the application as
specified under ¶ 8-302.14(C) and the change in operation is not APPROVED.
